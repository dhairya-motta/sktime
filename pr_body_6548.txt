Related to sktime/skpro#369

This PR adds `sample_weight` support to all probabilistic forecasting metrics.

Changes:
- Added `sample_weight` to the `_BaseProbaForecastingErrorMetric` and `_BaseDistrForecastingMetric` evaluation workflows.
- Extracted and validated weights using the standard `_apply_sample_weight_to_kwargs` method.
- Replaced unweighted `mean()` aggregations with `np.average(..., weights=sample_weight)` when weights are provided.
- Naturally inherited by all specific metrics (`PinballLoss`, `CRPS`, `LogLoss`, etc.) because their `_evaluate_by_index` functions successfully propagate `**kwargs`.
