**Reference Issues/PRs**
Fixes #8217. (Also related to #5445 and #8229)

**What does this implement/fix?**
This officially enables `capability:insample = True` for the `DynamicFactor` model.

Previously, if you tried to predict in-sample, the index mapping would break. Instead of rewriting everything, I just split the forecasting horizon (`fh`):
- For past dates (in-sample): It grabs the Kalman smoother estimates directly from `statsmodels`'s `fittedvalues`.
- For future dates (out-of-sample): It relies on the original `predict()` method.

Gluing them together this way bypasses all the index mapping bugs that the previous attempt (PR #8229) ran into.

**Does your contribution introduce a new dependency? If yes, which one?**
Nope.

**What should a reviewer concentrate their feedback on?**
Just looking for feedback on whether this bucket-splitting approach makes sense to you guys.

**Did you add any tests for the change?**
Not manually. Letting the existing `TestAllForecasters` CI suite handle the heavy lifting across all the different Pandas indices!
