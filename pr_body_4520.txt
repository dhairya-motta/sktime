[ENH] Add full probabilistic interface to ConformalIntervals

* Implements `_predict_proba` and `_predict_proba_series` for `ConformalIntervals`.
* Provides full support for probabilistic predictions, returning a `skpro` `Empirical` distribution.
* Evaluates correctly with `empirical`, `conformal`, `conformal_bonferroni`, and `empirical_residual` methods.
* Inherits directly from existing residuals matrices without modifying interval calculations.
* Includes necessary `pytest` unit-tests covering standard and parametrized setups.
